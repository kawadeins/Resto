========================

3. ACTION-FOCUSED INCIDENT CARDS
========================

Upgrade each incident card to clearly show:

- what happened (simple language)
- why it matters (impact)

- current status
- recommended action (clear and short)

- Issue: Premium not activated after valid payment  
- Impact: business visibility affected  
- Auto-action: attempted sync → success  
- Status: resolved  
- Action: no action required  

OR

- Action: review billing webhook configuration
