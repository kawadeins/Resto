MASTER RULES APPLY

Build a "Business Staff Management System" inside the existing RestoMaster business dashboard.

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT redesign the app.
Do NOT break existing systems.
Do NOT modify Founder dashboard.

This system is for business owners (restaurants, cafés, bars) to manage team members and access control.

========================
GOAL
========================

Allow business owners to:

- add staff members
- assign roles (owner / manager / staff)
- control permissions
- share dashboard access safely

This must be simple, secure, and production-ready.

========================
1. STAFF INVITE SYSTEM
========================

Allow owner to:

- invite staff via email (or simple identifier)
- send invitation
- accept invitation

After acceptance:
- staff gets access to business dashboard

========================
2. ROLES SYSTEM (CRITICAL)
========================

Define roles:

1. Owner:
- full control
- billing access
- can add/remove staff
- can change roles

2. Manager:
- can manage content
- can use boost tools
- can view analytics
- cannot access billing or owner settings

3. Staff:
- limited access
- view dashboard
- limited interaction
- no sensitive controls

========================
3. PERMISSION CONTROL
========================

Ensure:

- billing only visible to owner
- premium control only owner/manager
- critical systems protected
- no unauthorized access

========================
4. DASHBOARD SECTION
========================

Add section:

👉 "Team" or "Mitarbeiter"

Inside:

- list of staff
- role of each
- status (active / pending)
- invite button
- remove user
- change role

========================
5. SIMPLE UI (IMPORTANT)
========================

Keep it simple:

- name
- role
- status
- action buttons

No complex management system.

========================
6. SECURITY RULES
========================

- validate access server-side
- do NOT trust client role
- prevent privilege escalation
- ensure safe role updates

========================
7. ONBOARDING FLOW
========================

After business setup:

- show hint:
"Team hinzufügen und Zugriff teilen"

Encourage owner to add staff.

========================
8. GERMAN LANGUAGE
========================

All UI must be in German:

Examples:
- "Mitarbeiter hinzufügen"
- "Rolle ändern"
- "Einladung senden"
- "Manager"
- "Mitarbeiter"

========================
9. EDGE CASES
========================

Handle:

- duplicate invites
- removed users
- pending invites
- invalid access attempts

========================
10. DO NOT OVERCOMPLICATE
========================

No:
- HR system
- scheduling
- payroll

Keep:
- simple access control
- team management only

========================
FINAL GOAL
========================

Business owners can:

- share dashboard access
- manage team easily
- control permissions safely

The system must feel:

👉 simple  
👉 secure  
👉 professional  
👉 ready for real business use
