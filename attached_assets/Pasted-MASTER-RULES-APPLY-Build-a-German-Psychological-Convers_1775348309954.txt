MASTER RULES APPLY

Build a "German Psychological Conversion Layer" for the Premium Business system inside the existing RestoMaster platform.

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT redesign the whole app.
Do NOT break existing functionality.
Do NOT use manipulative dark patterns.
Do NOT use fake urgency.
All user-facing conversion copy must be in German only.

This system must support:
- Restaurants
- Cafés
- Bars / Nightlife venues

========================
GOAL
========================

Increase Premium conversion by improving the psychological structure of the premium experience.

The business owner should feel in a natural, professional, German-speaking way:

- Premium is valuable
- I am missing visibility without it
- This is made for my business
- 39,90€ is a small investment compared to potential return
- I should activate it now

This system is not about fake pressure.
It is about:
- clarity
- emotional business motivation
- local relevance
- intelligent persuasion

========================
1. PREMIUM PAGE PSYCHOLOGY
========================

Rework the Premium section so the business owner sees the information in the right order:

1. Problem
2. Opportunity
3. Value
4. Proof
5. Price
6. Action

Do NOT just show feature bullets.

The page should first make the owner feel:
- "Oh, I am missing visibility"
- "This could help me grow"

========================
2. GERMAN HOOKS (VERY IMPORTANT)
========================

Use strong but professional German hooks such as:

- "Mehr Sichtbarkeit für dein Geschäft"
- "Werde in deiner Nähe häufiger entdeckt"
- "Erreiche mehr Kunden genau dann, wenn sie suchen"
- "Nutze deine lokale Sichtbarkeit besser"

These hooks should appear in:
- premium page
- dashboard prompts
- trial messages
- upgrade states

========================
3. LOSS AVOIDANCE MESSAGING
========================

Use professional German messaging that highlights missed opportunity.

Examples:
- "Ohne Premium bleibt dein Geschäft weniger sichtbar"
- "Du verpasst potenzielle Reichweite in deiner Umgebung"
- "Andere Betriebe werden häufiger entdeckt"
- "Nutze dein lokales Potenzial besser"

Do NOT sound cheap or aggressive.

========================
4. ROI-STYLE PERSUASION
========================

Use German messages that frame Premium as business value:

Examples:
- "Schon ein zusätzlicher Kunde kann den Monatsbetrag rechtfertigen"
- "39,90€ im Monat für mehr lokale Sichtbarkeit"
- "Eine kleine Investition für mehr Reichweite und Vertrauen"

Keep it simple and believable.

========================
5. TRIAL CONVERSION LANGUAGE
========================

During the 14-day trial, use stronger progressive German conversion language:

Examples:
- "Deine Testphase läuft – nutze die volle Sichtbarkeit"
- "Noch 5 Tage kostenlos"
- "Behalte deine Reichweite auch nach der Testphase"
- "Jetzt Premium weiterführen für 39,90€ / Monat"

At trial end:
- "Deine Testphase ist beendet"
- "Aktiviere Premium, um sichtbar zu bleiben"

========================
6. BUSINESS-TYPE PERSONALIZATION
========================

Adapt copy by business type:

Restaurants:
- "Mehr Sichtbarkeit zur Mittags- und Abendzeit"
- "Erreiche Gäste zur richtigen Zeit"

Cafés:
- "Mehr Präsenz am Morgen und zur Kaffeezeit"
- "Werde für Frühstück und Coffee Breaks entdeckt"

Bars:
- "Mehr Sichtbarkeit am Abend und im Nachtleben"
- "Nutze die Aufmerksamkeit rund um Happy Hour und Nightlife"

========================
7. TRUST + STATUS LANGUAGE
========================

Make Premium feel prestigious in German:

Examples:
- "Verifizierter Business-Status"
- "Mehr Vertrauen bei neuen Kunden"
- "Professioneller Auftritt auf RestoMaster"

This should create emotional value, not only functional value.

========================
8. SMART MICROCOPY
========================

Improve small German texts around buttons, cards, and upgrade prompts.

Examples:
- "Jetzt sichtbar werden"
- "Mehr Reichweite freischalten"
- "Premium starten"
- "14 Tage kostenlos testen"
- "Sichtbarkeit erhöhen"

Avoid weak generic text.

========================
9. HIGH-INTENT UPGRADE MOMENTS
========================

Use stronger psychological copy at the best conversion moments:

- when trial is close to ending
- when owner sees analytics
- when owner sees missed visibility
- when owner opens boost tools
- when owner sees profile views or discovery metrics

Each moment should use short, relevant German persuasion.

========================
10. PROOF + PERFORMANCE LANGUAGE
========================

Show proof in German:

Examples:
- "Dein Profil wurde 124 Mal angesehen"
- "Mehr Nutzer haben dein Geschäft entdeckt"
- "Deine Sichtbarkeit ist während der Testphase gestiegen"

These messages should support payment decisions.

========================
11. CLEAN CTA STRUCTURE
========================

Use one strong primary CTA per screen.

Examples:
- "Jetzt 14 Tage kostenlos starten"
- "Premium aktivieren"
- "Für 39,90€ / Monat fortsetzen"

Do NOT overload with too many buttons.

========================
12. CONSISTENCY
========================

Ensure all Premium persuasion copy across the app is:
- in German only
- consistent
- professional
- psychologically strong
- adapted to business value

No mixed English.
No awkward translation.

========================
FINAL GOAL
========================

The Premium experience should make business owners feel in a natural German-speaking way:

- "Das bringt mir wirklich etwas"
- "Ich verliere Sichtbarkeit ohne Premium"
- "39,90€ ist sinnvoll"
- "Ich sollte das aktivieren"

The result should significantly improve Premium conversion while keeping the product professional and trustworthy.
