Implement a full "Predictive Notifications Engine" for both the customer app and business dashboard.

IMPORTANT:
- Do NOT redesign the app
- Do NOT break existing notifications, chat, plans, bookings, or business dashboard logic
- This is an intelligence + timing layer
- Notifications must feel useful, not spammy

==================================================
PRIMARY GOAL
==================================================

Build a smart notification system that predicts relevant moments and sends useful reminders/suggestions before the user has to think about them.

Support BOTH:
A. customer users
B. restaurant owners / business dashboard users

==================================================
1. CUSTOMER PREDICTIVE NOTIFICATIONS
==================================================

Send useful notifications for things like:
- upcoming group plan tomorrow
- reservation in 2 hours
- restaurant accepted/rejected a request
- a place matching the user's behavior is trending tonight
- relevant offer/discount based on preferences
- reminder to review a completed booking
- reminder to finish/share a saved plan

Examples:
- "Erinnerung: Dein Dinner ist morgen um 19:00"
- "Heute Abend passt dieses Restaurant perfekt zu dir"
- "Deine Reservierung wurde bestätigt"
- "Du kannst diese Reservierung jetzt bewerten"

==================================================
2. BUSINESS PREDICTIVE NOTIFICATIONS
==================================================

Send useful dashboard/business alerts such as:
- low wallet before a planned campaign
- boost likely useful at a specific time
- upcoming reservation load tomorrow
- new group request arrived
- critical review needs reply
- campaign underperforming
- scheduled action approaching

Examples:
- "Dein Guthaben reicht bald nicht mehr für geplante Kampagnen"
- "Heute könnte ein Abend-Boost sinnvoll sein"
- "Du hast morgen 5 Reservierungen"
- "Eine neue Gruppenanfrage wartet auf Bestätigung"

==================================================
3. USER BEHAVIOR-BASED LOGIC
==================================================

Use real behavior/history where available:
- nightlife preference
- café/food style
- plan habits
- booking habits
- saved places
- business campaign usage
- wallet usage
- review/booking patterns

Do NOT send random suggestions.

==================================================
4. TIME-BASED REMINDERS
==================================================

Support scheduled reminders such as:
- 24 hours before
- 3 hours before
- 2 hours before
- morning-of reminder
- planned send time for group reservation requests

==================================================
5. ACTIONABLE NOTIFICATIONS
==================================================

Notifications must be clickable and useful.

Examples:
- reservation accepted → opens booking
- group request response → opens plan/chat
- low wallet → opens top-up
- critical review → opens reviews
- recommended place → opens restaurant page

==================================================
6. PRIORITY LEVELS
==================================================

Classify notifications into:
- kritisch
- wichtig
- Info
- Vorschlag

Use this for:
- visual hierarchy
- badges
- notification center grouping

==================================================
7. BADGES + IN-APP CENTER
==================================================

Support:
- unread counters
- relevant badges
- notification center entries
- read/unread state

Applies to:
- customer side
- business dashboard side

==================================================
8. NO-SPAM RULES
==================================================

Prevent spam:
- deduplicate similar notifications
- do not send repeated weak suggestions
- suppress noisy low-value alerts
- prioritize useful, timely items only

==================================================
9. GERMAN UX COPY
==================================================

Use labels like:
- Erinnerung
- Bestätigt
- Abgelehnt
- Vorschlag für dich
- Heute besonders passend
- Aktion erforderlich
- Dein Guthaben ist fast leer

==================================================
10. FINAL VALIDATION
==================================================

Verify:
A. customer reminders work
B. customer suggestions feel relevant
C. business alerts are useful
D. low-value spam is avoided
E. notification click-through opens correct destination
F. no existing flows break

==================================================
FINAL RESULT
==================================================

RestoSmart should feel proactive and alive.

The user should feel:
- "The app reminds me at the right time"
- "The app suggests useful things"

The business owner should feel:
- "The dashboard warns me before problems happen"
- "The system helps me act earlier"
