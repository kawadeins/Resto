MASTER RULES APPLY

Build an "Auto Revenue Optimization Engine" inside the existing RestoMaster platform.

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT break existing functionality.
Do NOT redesign the app.
Do NOT create fake analytics.
This is a smart monetization optimization layer.

========================
GOAL
========================

Automatically help businesses:
- choose better budgets
- improve visibility performance
- increase conversions
- spend money more effectively

And increase platform revenue in a smart, non-annoying way.

========================
1. SMART PERFORMANCE ANALYSIS
========================

Continuously analyze for each business:

- impressions
- clicks
- visits
- bookings
- boost usage
- time-of-day performance
- category performance (restaurant/cafe/bar)

Detect patterns like:
- strong morning performance
- weak evening performance
- high clicks but low bookings
- high visibility but low engagement

========================
2. AUTO RECOMMENDATION ENGINE
========================

Generate smart suggestions inside dashboard:

Examples:
- “Your café performs best in the morning — boost 08:00–11:00”
- “Your bar is missing evening traffic — increase night visibility”
- “You are close to top results — increase budget by 5€”
- “Your listing gets views but low clicks — improve visibility position”

These should feel actionable.

========================
3. AUTO BUDGET SUGGESTIONS
========================

Suggest optimized budgets:

- increase budget when ROI is strong
- decrease or redirect when weak
- suggest daily budgets
- suggest campaign boosts

Examples:
- “+5€ can move you into top 3 results”
- “Shift budget from afternoon to evening”

========================
4. SMART BOOST OPTIMIZATION
========================

Recommend:
- best time slots
- best placement zones
- best days (weekday/weekend)

Examples:
- café → morning boost
- restaurant → lunch boost
- bar → night boost

========================
5. SOFT AUTO-ACTION (OPTIONAL)
========================

Allow optional auto-mode:

- “Auto Optimize Campaign”

If enabled:
- system adjusts budget slightly
- shifts timing
- improves performance gradually

Never:
- spend without limits
- override user control

========================
6. BUSINESS TYPE INTELLIGENCE
========================

Adapt by type:

Restaurants:
- lunch/dinner optimization
- booking conversion focus

Cafés:
- morning traffic
- work-hour visibility

Bars:
- nightlife boost
- evening crowd optimization

========================
7. REVENUE MOMENT DETECTION
========================

Detect high-value moments:

- high demand nearby
- trending zones
- live heat spikes
- social activity peaks

Then suggest:
👉 “Boost now — high demand in your area”

========================
8. ROI FEEDBACK LOOP
========================

Show business:

- what worked
- what didn’t
- what to change

Examples:
- “Night boost increased clicks by 35%”
- “Morning campaign generated most visits”

========================
9. FOUNDER REVENUE IMPACT
========================

Track:
- total boost revenue growth
- optimized campaigns vs manual
- best-performing strategies

========================
10. UI / UX
========================

Keep:
- simple suggestions
- clear language
- easy action buttons

Owner should feel:
👉 “This system helps me earn more”

========================
FINAL GOAL
========================

RestoMaster should actively help businesses:

- spend smarter
- earn more
- grow faster

And increase platform revenue naturally.
