MASTER RULES APPLY

Build a "Business Self-Serve Growth Loop" inside the existing RestoMaster platform.

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT redesign the app.
Do NOT break existing functionality.
Do NOT turn this into a complex CRM system.
All business-facing UI and messaging must be in German.

This system must support:
- restaurants
- cafés / coffee shops
- bars / nightlife venues

This system builds on:
- Premium system (39,90€ / month)
- 14-day trial
- Conversion Booster
- Conversion Analytics
- Auto-Optimization Loop
- Business Growth Engine

========================
GOAL
========================

Create a fully self-serve growth loop where business owners can:

- discover the platform
- join easily
- activate their business
- experience value quickly
- upgrade to Premium
- grow visibility
- reinvest in boosts

WITHOUT manual sales or external outreach.

========================
1. SELF-SERVE ENTRY (CRITICAL)
========================

Create a frictionless business entry flow.

A business owner should be able to:

- understand the platform quickly
- see value immediately
- join without complexity

German entry examples:
- "Geschäft hinzufügen"
- "Dein Business eintragen"
- "Jetzt starten"

Flow:
- select business type (Restaurant / Café / Bar)
- enter basic details
- continue directly to activation

========================
2. INSTANT VALUE EXPERIENCE
========================

Immediately after joining:

- show business profile live
- show how it appears in app
- show initial visibility potential

German examples:
- "So sehen Kunden dein Geschäft"
- "Dein Profil ist jetzt sichtbar"

This creates instant reward.

========================
3. QUICK ACTIVATION LOOP
========================

Guide the business owner through 3–4 simple steps:

- complete profile
- add images
- confirm business type
- activate visibility

Use German progress steps:
- "Profil vervollständigen"
- "Sichtbarkeit aktivieren"
- "Mehr Reichweite freischalten"

Keep it fast and clear.

========================
4. TRIAL ENTRY POINT
========================

Offer 14-day Premium trial at the right moment:

German CTA:
- "14 Tage kostenlos testen"

Show:
- what they unlock
- why it matters

Do NOT overwhelm early.
Introduce at high-value moment.

========================
5. VALUE LOOP (VERY IMPORTANT)
========================

During usage, continuously show value signals:

- profile views
- discovery exposure
- search appearances
- nearby visibility

German examples:
- "Dein Geschäft wurde 78 Mal angesehen"
- "Mehr Nutzer entdecken dein Profil"

This builds belief.

========================
6. GROWTH ACTION LOOP
========================

Guide business toward actions that improve visibility:

Examples:
- improve profile
- activate Premium
- use boost tools
- optimize presence

German prompts:
- "Erhöhe deine Sichtbarkeit"
- "Mehr Kunden erreichen"
- "Jetzt optimieren"

========================
7. PREMIUM UPGRADE LOOP
========================

Convert at the right moments:

- after value is seen
- near trial end
- after exposure proof
- when trying advanced features

German CTAs:
- "Premium aktivieren"
- "Für 39,90€ / Monat fortsetzen"

========================
8. BOOST / REINVEST LOOP
========================

After Premium activation:

- introduce boost tools
- allow spending on visibility
- connect spending to results

German examples:
- "Mehr Sichtbarkeit jetzt boosten"
- "Dein Geschäft hervorheben"

This creates continuous revenue.

========================
9. CONTINUOUS FEEDBACK LOOP
========================

Always show:

- what is working
- what can improve
- where opportunity exists

Examples:
- "Starke Nachfrage in deiner Umgebung"
- "Jetzt ist eine gute Zeit für mehr Sichtbarkeit"

========================
10. LOW-FRICTION UX
========================

Keep everything:

- simple
- fast
- intuitive

Avoid:
- complex onboarding
- too many forms
- heavy setup

Goal:
👉 join → activate → see value → upgrade

========================
11. TRUST + PROFESSIONAL FEEL
========================

Make system feel:

- serious
- business-oriented
- reliable

German tone:
- professional
- clear
- not aggressive
- not spammy

========================
12. DATA SUPPORT
========================

Track:

- business signups
- activation rate
- trial starts
- trial usage
- premium conversion
- boost usage
- reinvestment rate

========================
13. LOOP STRUCTURE (CORE)
========================

The system should naturally create this loop:

1. Discover platform  
2. Join easily  
3. Activate business  
4. See value  
5. Start trial  
6. Experience growth  
7. Convert to Premium  
8. Use boost tools  
9. See more results  
10. Reinvest  
11. Repeat  

========================
14. DO NOT OVERCOMPLICATE
========================

- no enterprise onboarding
- no sales funnels everywhere
- no aggressive upselling

Focus on:
- clarity
- speed
- value
- natural growth

========================
FINAL GOAL
========================

RestoMaster becomes a self-growing business platform where:

- businesses join on their own
- activate quickly
- see real value
- convert to Premium
- continue investing in visibility

The platform grows without heavy manual sales effort.

It becomes:

👉 a self-serve growth engine  
👉 a monetization loop  
👉 a scalable business system
