MASTER RULES APPLY

Build a "City Expansion Engine" inside the existing RestoMaster platform.

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT redesign the app.
Do NOT break existing functionality.
Do NOT overcomplicate the system.
All business-facing UI must remain in German.

This system must work with:
- Business Self-Serve Growth Loop
- Premium System (39,90€ / month)
- Boost / Competition Engine
- Heat Map / Live Activity
- Founder Command Center

========================
GOAL
========================

Enable RestoMaster to expand city-by-city in a controlled, strategic way.

The platform should:
- identify high-potential cities
- activate local supply (businesses)
- stimulate local demand (users)
- grow density
- convert businesses to Premium
- repeat in next city

This is a market domination system.

========================
1. CITY STRUCTURE (CORE)
========================

Define cities as core expansion units.

- business count
- premium penetration
- activity level
- user demand
- conversion metrics

Examples:
- Vienna
- Graz
- Linz
- Salzburg
- Innsbruck

2. CITY HEALTH SCORE
========================

- number of active businesses
- number of premium businesses
- user activity
- bookings / interactions
- boost usage
- conversion rate

Classify cities:
- Early Stage
- Growing
- Strong
- Dominant

========================
3. SUPPLY ACTIVATION (BUSINESSES)
========================

In early-stage cities:

- prioritize business onboarding
- show strong value messaging
- push trial (14 days)
- simplify onboarding

German messaging examples:
- "Jetzt dein Geschäft eintragen"
- "Werde in deiner Stadt sichtbar"

========================
4. DEMAND STIMULATION (USERS)
========================

Ensure users see value even with low supply:

- show best available places
- highlight early adopters
- emphasize discovery
- use smart offers where possible

Goal:
👉 users keep opening the app

5. LOCAL DENSITY STRATEGY
========================

Focus on density, not spread.

❌ spreading thin across many cities

Rules:
- reach critical mass in city
