========================
GOAL
========================

Adjust prices dynamically based on real demand, competition, and timing to:

- maximize platform revenue
- help businesses compete effectively
- keep pricing fair and transparent
- create natural urgency (not fake)

========================
1. DEMAND-BASED PRICING (CORE)
========================

Adjust boost pricing based on demand signals:

- number of businesses boosting in same area
- time of day demand
- live activity / heat map
- search activity
- user traffic
- competition density

Examples:
- high demand → higher cost
- low demand → lower cost

========================
2. TIME-BASED PRICING
========================

Prices vary depending on time:

Morning:
- cafés cheaper → encourage boost

Lunch:
- restaurants medium/high

Night:
- bars higher (high demand)

This aligns with real behavior.

========================
3. LOCATION-BASED PRICING
========================

Adjust pricing by area:

- city center → higher demand → higher price
- low-traffic areas → cheaper boosts

Example:
- Vienna center → higher cost
- outer districts → lower cost

========================
4. SLOT-BASED PRICING (IMPORTANT 🔥)
========================

Combine with limited slots:

- Top 3 slots → highest price
- Next positions → lower price
- standard boost → lowest

Example:
- Top #1 → premium price
- Top #2–3 → medium
- general boost → base

========================
5. REAL-TIME PRICE FEEDBACK
========================

Show business owners:

- current price
- demand level
- competition level

Examples:
- “High demand right now”
- “Prices increased due to activity”
- “Low competition — good time to boost”

========================
6. PRICE SUGGESTION ENGINE
========================

Suggest pricing actions:
