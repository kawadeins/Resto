MASTER RULES APPLY

Build a "Business Competition Engine" inside the existing RestoMaster platform.

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT redesign the whole app.
Do NOT break existing functionality.
Do NOT create a complex bidding/auction UI.
Do NOT manipulate users unfairly.

This is a controlled competition layer built on top of:
- Premium Business System (39,90€ / month)
- Boost / Sponsored Visibility System
- Dynamic Pricing Engine
- Heat Map / Live Activity
- Ranking / Behavior Engine

All business-facing UI and messaging must be in German.

========================
GOAL
========================

Create a system where businesses naturally compete for visibility in a controlled, fair, and profitable way.

The system should:
- increase demand for visibility
- increase boost spending
- make top positions valuable
- create natural competition without chaos

Businesses should feel:
- “I want to be seen more than others”
- “I should invest to stay visible”
- “This position matters”

========================
1. VISIBILITY TIERS (CORE)
========================

Define clear visibility tiers:

- Top Position (#1)
- Top 3 Positions
- Boosted Visibility
- Standard Listing

Higher tiers:
- get more exposure
- cost more
- are more competitive

Do NOT guarantee ranking — increase probability based on:
- boost
- relevance
- quality

========================
2. COMPETITION SIGNALS (VERY IMPORTANT)
========================

Show controlled competition signals to businesses.

German examples:

- "Hohe Konkurrenz in deiner Umgebung"
- "Andere Betriebe erhöhen aktuell ihre Sichtbarkeit"
- "Top-Positionen sind stark nachgefragt"
- "Jetzt sichtbar bleiben"

These signals must:
- feel real
- be based on actual activity
- create motivation

========================
3. LIMITED VISIBILITY SLOTS
========================

Create the feeling of limited space:

Examples:
- Top 3 positions are limited
- premium visibility zones are limited
- boosted slots per area are limited

German examples:
- "Begrenzte Top-Plätze verfügbar"
- "Nur wenige sichtbare Positionen in deiner Umgebung"

Do NOT fake scarcity.

========================
4. DYNAMIC COMPETITION FEEDBACK
========================

Show real-time feedback in dashboard:

- demand level (low / medium / high)
- competition level
- current visibility strength

German examples:
- "Hohe Nachfrage in deiner Region"
- "Starke Aktivität in deiner Umgebung"
- "Jetzt ist ein guter Zeitpunkt für mehr Sichtbarkeit"

========================
5. POSITION VALUE AWARENESS
========================

Make businesses understand that higher positions matter.

Examples:
- more visibility
- more discovery
- more clicks
- more customers

German examples:
- "Top-Positionen werden häufiger gesehen"
- "Mehr Sichtbarkeit bedeutet mehr Reichweite"
- "Höhere Platzierung = mehr Aufmerksamkeit"

========================
6. COMPETITION-DRIVEN BOOSTING
========================

Encourage businesses to invest in visibility:

Examples:
- increase budget
- activate boost
- maintain position

German examples:
- "Jetzt Sichtbarkeit erhöhen"
- "Bleib in den Top-Ergebnissen sichtbar"
- "Mehr Reichweite durch Boost"

This must feel like:
👉 smart opportunity  
not pressure

========================
7. FAIRNESS RULES (CRITICAL)
========================

The system must remain fair:

- relevance still matters
- quality still matters
- ratings still matter
- premium boosts visibility but does not fully override quality

Do NOT allow:
- low-quality businesses dominating purely by money
- broken ranking trust

========================
8. BUSINESS-TYPE COMPETITION
========================

Adapt competition by type:

Restaurants:
- lunch / dinner competition

Cafés:
- morning / coffee rush competition

Bars:
- evening / nightlife competition

German examples:
- "Hohe Nachfrage zur Mittagszeit"
- "Starker Wettbewerb am Morgen"
- "Hohe Aktivität im Nachtleben"

========================
9. HEAT MAP INTEGRATION
========================

Use live activity signals:

- high-activity zones = higher competition
- low-activity zones = cheaper opportunity

German examples:
- "Hotspot in deiner Nähe"
- "Starke Aktivität in diesem Bereich"
- "Jetzt gute Chance für mehr Sichtbarkeit"

========================
10. PREMIUM ADVANTAGE
========================

Premium businesses should:

- get better access to visibility tools
- have stronger presence in competition zones
- appear more often when relevant

But:
- must still respect ranking logic

========================
11. UI / UX STYLE
========================

Use:

- clean indicators
- small badges
- simple competition levels
- no complex bidding screens

Avoid:
- ad manager complexity
- confusing graphs
- overwhelming data

========================
12. FOUNDER INSIGHT
========================

Track:

- competition intensity
- boost spending increase
- top slot demand
- conversion under competition
- revenue per area

Show inside Founder Command Center:
- most competitive cities
- most competitive business types
- highest revenue zones

========================
13. DO NOT OVERCOMPLICATE
========================

- no real-time auctions
- no bidding wars UI
- no chaotic system

Focus on:
- controlled competition
- simple signals
- natural motivation
- increased revenue

========================
FINAL GOAL
========================

RestoMaster should create a healthy, controlled competition where:

- businesses want better visibility
- top positions become valuable
- demand increases
- boost spending increases
- revenue grows

The system should feel:

👉 fair  
👉 competitive  
👉 profitable  
👉 smart
