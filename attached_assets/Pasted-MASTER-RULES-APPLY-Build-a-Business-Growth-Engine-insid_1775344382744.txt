MASTER RULES APPLY

Build a "Business Growth Engine" inside the existing RestoMaster platform.

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT redesign the app.
Do NOT add random unrelated features.
Do NOT turn this into a CRM or marketing automation monster.

This is a business acquisition and conversion system for:
- restaurants
- cafés / coffee shops
- bars / nightlife venues

========================
GOAL
========================

Make RestoMaster naturally attract, convert, and grow business owners inside the platform.

The system should help businesses understand:

- why they should join
- what value they get
- how much visibility they are missing
- why premium is worth paying for

The goal is to reduce dependence on manual sales and create internal business growth.

========================
1. BUSINESS ENTRY PATH (CRITICAL)
========================

Create a clear business-owner entry path inside the app.

The app must communicate that it is not only for customers, but also for business owners:
- restaurants
- cafés
- bars

There should be a clear way for a business owner to understand:
- this platform supports my business type
- I can join
- I can be discovered
- I can grow here

========================
2. VALUE EXPLANATION LAYER
========================

Add a strong value explanation for business owners.

Explain clearly that businesses can get:
- more nearby visibility
- discovery in search and homepage
- map exposure
- smart offers exposure
- social/live visibility
- premium trust badge
- boost and promoted placement options
- analytics and growth insight

This value explanation should adapt by business type:

Restaurants:
- bookings / lunch / dinner traffic

Cafés:
- coffee rush / breakfast / work traffic

Bars:
- nightlife / evening / happy hour traffic

========================
3. MISSED OPPORTUNITY SYSTEM
========================

Show business owners what they are missing if they are not premium or not active.

Examples:
- “Your business could appear higher nearby”
- “You are missing local visibility”
- “Nearby users are searching right now”
- “Unlock stronger map visibility”
- “Your business could benefit from morning/lunch/night boosts”

This should create real motivation, not fake pressure.

========================
4. BUSINESS CLAIM / ACTIVATE FLOW
========================

Allow a business owner to:
- claim their business
or
- activate their business profile
or
- join as a new business

This should be simple and direct.

Flow:
- identify business
- select business type
- enter basic details
- understand platform value
- move into setup / premium path

========================
5. BUSINESS-TYPE ADAPTATION
========================

The system must support all business types equally:

- restaurant
- café
- bar

And adapt messaging/examples accordingly.

Do NOT make the system restaurant-only.

========================
6. GROWTH SIGNALS FOR BUSINESSES
========================

Where possible, show growth signals such as:
- people nearby
- searches in this area
- relevant time-based traffic
- visibility opportunity
- nearby demand by time of day

Examples:
- “Strong coffee demand in your area this morning”
- “Nightlife activity is high nearby”
- “Lunch visibility is valuable in this zone”

These signals should feel data-backed, not fake.

========================
7. PREMIUM CONVERSION PATH
========================

Guide business owners toward premium in a clean and smart way.

Premium should feel like:
- growth tool
- visibility engine
- business advantage

Not just:
- subscription
- locked dashboard

Show premium as:
- more exposure
- more discovery
- better trust
- more business opportunities

========================
8. BOOST / ADS ENTRY
========================

Inside the business growth path, make it clear that premium businesses can also use:
- boost visibility
- sponsored placement
- promoted local visibility

This should reinforce monetization:
- subscription + boost budget

========================
9. TRUST + PROFESSIONALISM
========================

The business growth engine must feel:
- serious
- premium
- useful
- trustworthy

Avoid:
- spammy upsell feel
- cheap ad language
- fake urgency

Business owners must feel:
“This platform can really help me grow.”

========================
10. FOUNDER INSIGHT CONNECTION
========================

Connect this system to founder insights where relevant.

Track:
- business profile claims
- business activations
- premium conversions
- business-type conversion rates
- city-level business adoption

This should help the founder understand:
- which business types convert best
- which cities show strongest adoption
- what growth messages work best

========================
11. DO NOT OVERCOMPLICATE
========================

- no full sales CRM
- no heavy outbound automation
- no enterprise onboarding suite

Focus on:
- clear entry
- clear value
- clear growth path
- premium conversion
- business self-onboarding

========================
FINAL GOAL
========================

RestoMaster should include a real Business Growth Engine that helps:
- restaurants
- cafés
- bars

discover that the platform is valuable for them,
join it,
activate their profile,
and convert into premium customers.

The platform should begin to attract business owners naturally, not only through manual outreach.
