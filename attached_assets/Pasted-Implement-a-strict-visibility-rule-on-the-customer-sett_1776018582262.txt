Implement a strict visibility rule on the customer settings page.

IMPORTANT:
- Do NOT redesign the app
- Do NOT remove the Premium section
- Premium must remain visible and clear
- This task is ONLY about hiding the top "Konto" business-owner account block from normal users

==================================================
PRIMARY GOAL
==================================================

The top "Konto" section on the settings page must NOT be visible to normal customer users.

It should only appear for users who already have active business access, such as:
- active Premium / business account
- restaurant owner account
- approved business-side user
- valid owner/business identity in the app

==================================================
1. KEEP PREMIUM SECTION VISIBLE
==================================================

The "Business Premium freischalten" section must stay visible to normal users.

Do NOT hide Premium.
Do NOT remove the upsell / CTA to become a business owner.

This section is important and should remain clearly available.

==================================================
2. HIDE ONLY THE TOP KONTO BLOCK FOR NORMAL USERS
==================================================

The first "Konto" block at the top of the page must be hidden for normal customer users.

This includes the block showing things like:
- avatar
- display name
- email
- account identity card
- business-side account presentation

A normal user should not see this owner/business account card.

==================================================
3. SHOW KONTO ONLY AFTER BUSINESS/PREMIUM ACCESS
==================================================

The top "Konto" business-account block should only render if the user is truly eligible, for example:
- active business premium
- active owner/business account
- approved business-side account
- whatever existing product rule defines "business user"

If the user is a normal customer:
- do not render this section at all

==================================================
4. NORMAL USER SETTINGS MUST STAY CLEAN
==================================================

For normal customer users, the settings page should show only normal user items such as:
- Benachrichtigungen
- Datenschutz
- Hilfe
- Abmelden
- other customer-safe settings

No business account card at the top.

==================================================
5. PREMIUM CTA MUST STAY STRONG
==================================================

The Premium / business CTA must remain visible and attractive for normal users.

The user should still clearly see that they can upgrade / activate the business side.

But they should NOT already see the business owner account block before they qualify.

==================================================
6. BUSINESS USER EXPERIENCE
==================================================

For users who already are business/premium users:
- show the top Konto block
- keep their business-side account card visible
- keep the settings/business access flow working correctly

==================================================
7. DO NOT BREAK ANYTHING
==================================================

Make sure this change does NOT break:
- customer settings
- premium upgrade CTA
- business premium activation flow
- owner/business access
- business dashboard entry
- normal user settings UX

==================================================
8. FINAL VALIDATION
==================================================

Verify:

A. Normal customer user:
- does NOT see the top Konto business block
- still sees Premium CTA clearly

B. Active business/premium user:
- DOES see the top Konto block
- still sees correct business account access

C. No mixed-account confusion remains

==================================================
FINAL RESULT
==================================================

The settings page must behave like this:

Normal customer:
- no business Konto block
- yes Premium CTA

Business/premium user:
- yes business Konto block
- yes business access

Hide only the business account card.
Keep Premium visible.
